# Meta Ads Copy

## Compliance Direction

Use broad, benefit-led language. Avoid implying that Meta knows personal attributes, relationship status, private behavior, age, or sensitive preferences.

Use "for men who want..." sparingly and avoid direct "Are you..." framing in ads.

## Creative Direction

Safe image ideas:

- Camila in a professional wellness setting, fully clothed.
- Close-up of hands holding a notebook with warm lighting.
- Calm spa-like room with towels, candles, and no bodies.
- Neutral portrait of Camila smiling, no suggestive pose.

Avoid:

- exposed skin
- massage table body shots
- couples in bed
- sexualized facial expressions
- before/after claims
- fake testimonials

## Hooks To Avoid

- Are you lonely?
- Is your relationship failing?
- Want to satisfy her?
- Men over 45 need this.
- Fix your private life tonight.
- Secret technique women crave.
- Explicit or adult wording of any kind.
