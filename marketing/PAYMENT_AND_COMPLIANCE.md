## Payment Recommendation

Use PayPal as the safest first option for international buyers if Stripe review risk is high.

Stripe may work for general wellness education, but it has strict restrictions around adult content and adult services. The offer must not be presented as sexual services, explicit content, or a sexually oriented massage business.

## Stripe Risk Notes

Official Stripe guidance says it restricts adult content and services, including sexually explicit materials and sexually oriented massage parlors. That means the checkout, product name, landing page, and delivery content should be framed as wellness education, body awareness, presence, and massage instruction.

Source:

- https://support.stripe.com/questions/restricted-business-list-faqs
- https://stripe.com/legal/restricted-businesses

## PayPal Risk Notes

PayPal can also restrict adult-oriented products or services depending on presentation and account review. Keep the product educational, non-explicit, and clearly digital.
